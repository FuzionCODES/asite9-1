About the Film

Released on September 9, 2009 (09/09/09), 9 is a post-apocalyptic computer-animated film directed by Shane Acker and produced by Tim Burton and Timur Bekmambetov. Set in a world where humanity was wiped out by intelligent machines, nine ragdoll-like beings ("stitchpunks") must piece together the mystery of their creation to protect the spark of life.

The Talisman & The Machine

The mysterious Talisman binds the souls of the creations to the physical world. Constructed using three key alchemy symbols, it possesses the power to unlock the Fabrication Machine—or seal it away forever.
